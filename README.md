# pairatroopers highscore privacy

Deleting your account and data – Paira Troopers

You can request the deletion of your account and associated data by sending an email to:

neingamingstudios@gmail.com

Please include the following in your email:
- The name of the app: Paira Troopers
- The email address of the Google account you use to play

We will delete the high scores and gameplay data linked to your account.

Google may retain certain data as required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

# privacy

The app uses only your user ID to place you on the global leaderboard. Apart from your score, no other information is stored or collected. The app does not track anything, contains no advertisements or telemetry, and does not collect any other data from you. The data is not shared with third parties.
